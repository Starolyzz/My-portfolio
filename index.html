About

I'm a freelance graphic designer based in Great Falls, VA. I trained formally, then picked up more from working inside actual corporate and agency settings, where the deadlines are real and nobody's grading on a curve.

I'm strongest in 2D design and brand identity work — turning a rough idea into a visual concept people recognize. I like starting from a blank page and I like switching media so I don't get stuck in one groove (psst — I'm learning motion design right now), because as they say, "a jack of all trades is a master of none, but oftentimes better than a master of one."
