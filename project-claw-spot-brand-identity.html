The Brief

A brand identity. From nothing. In one day.

The Claw Spot is a claw machine arcade in Houston, launching from scratch and needing a full visual identity before opening. I was brought on to design the logo with one condition: done within 24 hours. It had to feel fun and high-energy, but still polished enough to hold up on signage and merch, not just a phone screen.

That logo ended up anchoring everything that came after it, including the hiring push and the grand-opening posts.

The Process

Character-first, then system.

With only 24 hours on the clock, I committed to one idea fast: a badge emblem built around a kawaii mascot, a cheerful claw machine character with big sparkly eyes, rosy cheeks, and mechanical arms. The circular badge referenced arcade tokens, with "THE" arching over the top and "CLAW SPOT" in bubbly, inflated lettering along the bottom. Periwinkle blue, coral pink, and gold carried straight into the hiring and grand-opening posts afterward, so the mascot stayed the face of the brand from day one.

The Outcome

The mark that started everything.

It shipped on time, and everything else — the grand-opening materials, the hiring posts, the whole social presence I went on to build — grew out of that one logo. It later got manufactured into a backlit sign inside the venue itself, glowing over the machines. Honestly, the thing I'm proudest of isn't the sign. It's that a first-day decision, made in a few hours, still held up months later.

A tight deadline is annoying in the moment and clarifying in hindsight. I didn't have time to second-guess myself, so I didn't.
